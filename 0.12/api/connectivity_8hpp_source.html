45// KiCad 10's item graph (pcbnew/connectivity, CN_VISITOR): items of any nets are linked when their copper touches
46// on a common layer, except two items that cannot change net (pads, zone fills, free vias) on different nets.
47// Tracks, arcs, vias and board copper graphics can change net. Footprint graphics and copper text are not part of it.
48// Pads of one net keep the stricter pad-to-pad rule of compute_connectivity (centre inside the other pad, or one
49// footprint's overlapping pads). Zone fills are left out of `adj`: testing every item against every large fill
50// polygon is slow, and only a few questions need them, so those ask zones_touching.
51struct ItemGraph {
52 std::vector<std::vector<int>> adj; // neighbours of each copper item, ascending (empty for items outside the graph)
53};
