Detailed Description

Connector edge pull for the placer (doc 15 §5.2 edge; CONN-01, USB2-11, DISP-02), opt-in (tracemaker-place –edge-attraction): the anchor of every instance with a connector edge rule (mode soft/on, not advisory, not disabled by the user), once per footprint, never a locked part and never a pin header or socket (board-to-board and jumper headers, CONN-01 note).

body holds the courtyard outline points (else pad corners), absolute; the placer pulls the body point nearest to the board outline toward the outline.
