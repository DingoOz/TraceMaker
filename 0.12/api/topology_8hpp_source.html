54// Decision D25 (doc 04 §2): a two-pad capacitor (C*) between a supply net and a ground net is tied to the nearest
55// pad, in the input placement, of an IC (U*, IC*) on the same supply; ties keep the lowest pad index.
56// `usable(fp)` filters footprints (the placer passes "has a part"). With `generalised` (doc 15 ic_decoupling),
57// the supply may also be an analog supply/reference name (VREF, AREF, VCAP, ...) or a net that reaches an IC pad
58// whose pin name is a supply pin, and the ground may be a net reaching an IC ground pin by name.
59struct DecapTie {
60 int cap_fp = -1;
61 int cap_pad = -1; // the capacitor's supply pad
62 int ic_pad = -1; // the IC pad it is tied to
63};
64std::vector<DecapTie> decap_ties(const model::Board& b, const std::function<bool(int)>& usable, bool generalised);
