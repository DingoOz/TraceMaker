Detailed Description

(D) Legalisation (design doc 04 §3 D): Tetris-style greedy, largest parts first, each part moved to the nearest legal position (minimum Euclidean displacement over a lattice searched in rings) from its target.

Candidates are screened with the conservative occupancy raster and always confirmed by the exact test.
