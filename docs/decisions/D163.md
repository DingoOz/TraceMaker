# D163 — Two rescue passes by default: relax from the plain routes, then from nothing

## Context

Doc 20 §2.2, step 2 of the plan for 100 % routed boards. The relax rescue (D160) routes a board left open again from nothing. A warm
start, relax started from the plain run's routes, was not measured then. Stop rule (doc 20 §2.2): keep the warm start only if it wins
on the sum and on clean boards, or matches them in less time.

## Decision

- **Both, warm first** (`--rescue-mode both`, the default; doc 05 §71). The warm pass starts relax from the plain run's routes
  (`RouterOptions::start_routes`). If connections are still open, the fresh pass routes from nothing. Each pass has its own budget
  (D160) and is kept only if better than the best so far.
- `--rescue-mode warm|fresh` runs one pass. `--no-rescue` still turns the rescue off.

## Alternatives

- **Warm only:** +51 against fresh (14 better, 5 worse) at 22 % less CPU, but 4 clean boards against 5. It fails the stop rule by one
  clean board (serial_gw and teensy-fx each end one short).
- **Fresh only (D160):** misses the warm pass's gains (poncho +6, MonApollo +9, Aleste +17, Teensy_test_layout complete).
- **Fresh first, then warm from the fresh result:** a warm start needs routes legal under the real rules. A relax result is, but the
  measured warm pass started from the plain run's, so that is what was adopted.

## Consequences

- On the 33 failing PCBench tier B-D boards, the best of the two per board is 16,280 routed against 16,208 (+72), with 6 clean boards
  against 5. CPU on boards left open is about 133,000 s against 110,958 (+20 %). Projected per board from the two measured arms: the
  fresh pass is independent of the warm one, so "both" is their maximum.
- A board left open after the warm pass is routed three times. A default (time-limited) run can take three times `--time`.
- Records: `bench/records/complete-routing/runs` (`warm-vs-fresh.md`, `best-of-both.md`). Docs: doc 05 §71, doc 20 §2.2 and §4,
  README. Test: `tests/integration/round_options.py`.
