# D192 — Exact courtyard outlines and closure moves that keep the input's overlaps

## Context

Placement closure (D190, doc 04 §11) stopped with "no candidates" on serial_gw, phone_amp and memsarray (`bench/records/m24-closure`).
Every part moved by a closure arm had to be free of every conflict. These hand placements have 21 (serial_gw) and 43 (phone_amp)
conflicts in the placer's model, so no part next to the failed connections could move. M24 (doc 11) asked for exact courtyard polygons in
the legality model, and for a rule that lets a part in an existing overlap move when the overlap does not grow, judged by KiCad.

Two measurements decided the shape of the change:

- The model's courtyard was the convex hull of all of a footprint's courtyard graphics per side. KiCad's is the closed outlines those
  graphics form. On the three boards the exact outlines remove 1 of the conflicts (phone_amp J5/F1); the rest are real. On serial_gw the
  RFM69 module U4 has a courtyard on B.CrtYd as well as F.CrtYd, and the designer put capacitors C1-C7 on the back under it.
- The bench's DRC judge (`bench/drc_judge.py`) matches errors by type and item positions. A footprint error's position is the
  footprint's, so a moved part that still overlaps the same neighbour counts as a new error. That is why D190 required a moved part to be
  conflict-free.

## Decision

1. **Exact courtyards, on by default** (`ExtractOptions::exact_courtyards`, `place/problem.cpp`). Each side's courtyard graphics are
   chained into closed outlines: lines and arcs end to end within 20 µm, with rectangles, polygons and circles already closed. Each
   outline is a polygon. A side that does not close (KiCad's "malformed courtyard") or holds a curve keeps the convex hull. The inset test
   against the board edge still uses each outline's hull (it needs a convex shape). `tracemaker-place --hull-courtyards` restores the old
   model.
2. **`--closure-keep-overlaps`, off by default** (`ClosureOptions::keep_overlaps`). A closure move may put a part where it overlaps only
   parts whose courtyards really overlapped it in the input (`keepable_pairs`: not merely within the courtyard clearance), and only by
   courtyard: holes and copper must stay clear of them (`Legality::hard_conflict`), because KiCad reports those by position. No new pair
   of overlapping parts appears. Such moves rank after every strictly legal move of their arm. The window arm uses the same test
   (`Metrics::new_pairs`).
4. **Margin lines hold pads off** like the board edge (`Problem::margins`, with their stroke), as KiCad's copper-to-edge check does.
3. **The judge reports both.** `drc_judge.added_errors(..., courtyard_pairs=True)` matches `courtyards_overlap` by the pair of footprints
   it names, wherever they are; a malformed courtyard and a pad too close to the edge are matched by the footprint or pad they name.
   `bench/run.py` records `place_added_pairs` and `place_error_gate_pairs` next to the position-based
   `place_added` and `place_error_gate`, which stay the gate.

## Alternatives

- **Tolerate any existing overlap of a moved part** (D190's first version): KiCad then reported new errors on 1Bitsy, because the part
  gained new neighbours. The pair rule forbids exactly that.
- **Require the overlap area not to grow:** KiCad reports pairs, not areas, so area is not what the judge sees. It would also need a
  polygon intersection the legality model does not have.
- **Repair the input first** (`--repair-input`, D75): that moves the designer's parts to remove overlaps they chose, before closure. It
  is a different question from closing the routing.

## Consequences

Measured on the 14 closure boards at D190's recipe (`bench/records/m24`): 32 open, 4 complete, 3 clean, the same totals as D190.
serial_gw is now clean (its move needs the keep rule); 58r-mxalps is lost, because a newly legal move came up early and the loop's time
limit arrived before D190's completing move. phone_amp gets moves (5 → 4 open). memsarray had moves under D190 too; with 2 movable
parts it exhausts them. With footprint errors matched by footprint, no board gains a KiCad error from placement. A first version that
kept any pair the model reported, with no copper test, made 1Bitsy and phone_amp gain real errors (a short, a new overlap, pads beside the
Margin lines); the copper test, `keepable_pairs` and the Margin lines are the fixes.

The milestone's gate is not met (58r-mxalps worse at equal routes), so `--closure-keep-overlaps` stays off. Exact courtyards and the
Margin lines are on, because they are what KiCad checks.
