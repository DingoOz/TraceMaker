# D103 — Clearance continuation ramp with warm starts

## Decision

Clearance continuation: `--ramp-from C0 --ramp-step S --ramp-rounds N [--ramp-cycle K --ramp-stall M]` runs the portfolio rounds under clearances lowered by (board - c) for c = C0, C0+S, … below the board's, each stage warm-started from the previous routes that are legal under its rules (`--state-recheck`: legal against fixed copper, then greedy vertex cover of the conflict graph, then the strict exact check); the last stage and the returned result are always under the board's rules, and with `--ramp-cycle` the last stage can go back K stages when it stalls ("breathing")

## Alternatives

Route straight at the board's rules (plateau at 1–6 open on six srj20 boards after hours); tighten the rules in the router itself without staged warm starts

## Reason

At the board's rules the search from scratch stalls although solutions exist (sample048 completes at 0.085 mm when warm-started from the 0.06/0.07 solutions, 30 min from scratch at 0.08 leave one open); the solution at 0.085 uses only 4 double-used via gaps and 43 % of the top pad gaps, so the plateau is search, not capacity. Test: `clearance ramp` (two cases) in tests/test_txn.cpp
