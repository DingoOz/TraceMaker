# D196 — route --finish: rungs on top of one another towards a complete board

## Context

Rule fit from nothing (D194) made 3 to 5 of 33 boards worse: with other rules the search takes another course, and a relaxation that
only adds room can still end with more open (poncho 33 → 45 from softer mask openings alone). A warm start (`--warm-pcb`) takes over only
part of the earlier routes (58r-mxalps: 0 open → 8). The request was for complete boards, with rules relaxed and compute spent as needed.

## Decision

`route --finish` (`run_finish`, `src/app/route_job.cpp`; doc 20 §2.12) runs rungs, each with the board the rung before wrote as its
input, so what is routed stays and only the open connections are searched:

1. the run as given;
2. fitted rules (`--fit-rules-from` the board as given, or the reference the job names) with `--repair on --repair-select both
   --repair-focus`;
3. with `--finish-fresh`: the board as given with the fitted rules, routed from the start (not on top);
4. with `--finish-masks`: the same as rung 2 with `--soft-pad-masks` (D197);
5. one rung per `--finish-layers` entry (e.g. `2,4`): the same with that many copper layers more than the board has.

It stops at the first rung with nothing open. A rung that does not lower the open count is discarded. The summary's `finish.rungs`
lists each rung's open count, layers and fitted rules. The rules are always fitted to the board as given, never to an earlier rung's
copper. Off by default; needs `--output`.

## Alternatives

- Fit from nothing and keep the better of two runs: twice the work for the best of 890 → 700; on top reaches 597 with one more run.
- A warm start: not monotone (above).
- Layers first: more layers are a cost to the user; rules the board already breaks are not. Layers are opt-in and last.

## Consequences

`bench/records/finish-2026-11`. As one command with `--finish-masks --finish-layers 2,4` on 29 of the 30 boards open at base: 881 open
become 219 (127 of them d20) and eight complete, KiCad 0 unconnected on each. With the rungs run by hand before the command existed: On the 33 failing PCBench boards: rung 2 takes 890 open to 597 and 3 complete to 7; +2 layers
8 complete, +4 layers 10 (Aleste 250 → 0). Three times the budget instead: 731 and 6. The best arm per board, the ladder on top of the
60 M run included, completes 16 of 33 (245 open, 129 of them d20). Limits: on top is not always the better order (PCIE completes fitted
from nothing, not on top: the opt-in fresh rung completes it, 30 → 2 → 0); pins walled in by an earlier rung's copper stay walled in when layers are added, unless a repair window frees
them (d20: nothing gained from layers).
