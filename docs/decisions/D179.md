# D179 — Pad-to-plane bound pays a via off the plane layer; causes fills zones as route does

## Context

M22 item 4 (doc 18 §18, D155) was "informed pad-to-plane searches: a lower bound to the net's fill (plus a via when off the plane
layer), the late proof, planning to nearby same-net copper; `causes` fills zones as `route` does". On 2026-10-29 the bound (octile
distance to the plane's box), the proof and the fallback were built as `--plane-informed` (doc 05 §63), without the via term, and
`causes` was not changed; the option was not measured on PCBench.

## Decision

- With `--plane-informed`, the A* heuristic of a pad-to-plane search adds `via_cost` for a state on another layer than the plane's.
  Admissible and consistent: such a search has no target cells but the plane on its one layer, and every layer change costs at least
  `via_cost` (through: `via_cost` plus a non-negative extra; blind or micro: 3/2 of it). Off by default, as the rest of the option.
- `tracemaker causes` fills the copper zones that have no fill, in memory, for the board's copper, and routes its copies with soft zones,
  as `route` does (`route_job.cpp`, doc 05 §54). On by default; `--no-fill-planes` keeps the old behaviour and `--soft-zones` makes the
  fills soft without filling. The JSON gets `zones_filled` and `soft_zones`.

## Alternatives

- **A bound to the nearest fill polygon, not its box.** Tighter, but a point-to-polygon distance per heuristic call; the measurement
  below says the bound does not change a decision on the gate set, so a tighter one was not built.
- **Leaving `causes` reading the board as written.** A board routed from unfilled zones comes back unfilled (the output keeps the zones as
  written), so every plane connection looked open and got a wrong cause.

## Consequences

On the M22 gate set (33 `pcbench-bd-fail` boards, 10 M work, 8 variants) `--plane-informed` with the via term gives byte-identical
boards to the base (`bench/records/m22-plane`): item 4 does nothing for the gate. All eight M22 items are now built and off by default;
the best arm cuts open connections by 3.5 % against the 15 % gate, which is not met. On WholeBoard's PoE switch `causes` now fills two
unfilled zones and its baseline changes from 90 to 100 routed. Doc 05 §63, doc 18 §18, roadmap M22.
