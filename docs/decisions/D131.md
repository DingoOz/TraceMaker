# D131 — `--package-tunnels` under dense packages

## Decision

`--package-tunnels`: stub, via and back-layer run between two perimeter pins of one net on a dense package, placed before the lanes (doc 05 §52)

## Context

In every best state of the EtherCAT study the last open connection was the core rail's pin 38, cut off from its trunk under the package; the designer's solution is a tunnel (review item 5)

## Consequences and results

Off by default; every piece passes the exact check and nothing is placed unless the whole tunnel does; costs connections at the loose first-round rules, to be judged in the closure loop
