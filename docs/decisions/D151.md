# D151 — Clearances resolved in KiCad's order; copper graphics compared as KiCad compares their nets

## Context

After D150, vme-wren still had 425 clearance and 20 shorting reports from the engine's DRC that KiCad 10.0.6 does not make:

- **Clearance.** The engine floored every clearance at the board minimum (0.125 mm), but the board's custom rule allows 0.1 mm under
  the FPGA and the DDR. KiCad's `DRC_ENGINE::EvalRules` returns a matching custom rule's value before the board-minimum floor, and
  checks a pad's local override before any custom rule.
- **Shorting.** These were between shapes of one footprint: copper lines that meet in the outline of each U.FL connector. In KiCad a
  footprint's copper shape has no net at all (`PCB_SHAPE::IsConnected`) and nets are compared by pointer, so two footprint shapes are
  never tested against each other, and neither are two board-level items without a net.

Doc 08 §3.2, records in `bench/records/d151-clearance`.

## Decision

- **`RuleEngine::clearance` in KiCad's order**:
  1. a local override (pad, then footprint), floored at the board minimum;
  2. else the last matching custom rule, final with no floor;
  3. else the net class, with the coupled-pair relaxation and a zone's own clearance, floored at the board minimum.
- **`hole_clearance` and `hole_to_hole`**: a matching custom rule replaces the board minimum instead of being raised to it.
- **Copper graphics**:
  - two footprint graphics (of one footprint or of two) are not checked against each other;
  - a board graphic is not checked against another board item without a net (graphic, pad, track or via).
- **Hole names**: a hole without copper is named "NPTH pad of <reference>" in reports, as KiCad names it, and is listed in its
  `hole_clearance` reports.

## Alternatives

- **Keep the floor as a safety margin.** The router would keep 0.125 mm where the designer allowed 0.1 mm under a BGA. Fan-outs
  there need the rule, and the DRC would keep reporting errors that KiCad does not.
- **Port KiCad's pointer and UUID-order logic for graphics in full.** Whether an overlap is a short or a clearance error depends on
  which of the two items has the larger UUID, and the engine keeps no UUIDs. The two rules above are the ones the fixtures need.

## Consequences

- vme-wren matches KiCad on every routing-relevant type, and jetson still does. Of the other 15 demo boards 12 match as before;
  microwave's shorts go from 10 to 8 (KiCad 6), One-Air-Max and RoyalBlue differ as before.
- Rule parity: 239 cases, five new, all matching KiCad. The D149 build fails all five.
- Among the fixtures only vme-wren has a rule below its board minimum, and no board with custom clearance rules has local overrides,
  so no bench board routes differently.
- On merge with D139 (branch `cache-safe-custom-rules`), its check that the per-class cache judges custom rules as the exact check
  does must use this order.
