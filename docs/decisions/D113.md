# D113 — `failure` event explains what rejected the path

## Decision

A `failure` event says what rejected the path (issue #8): the attempt's track width, clearance and the narrowest legal width, the bottleneck of the best way from the closest approach (`gap_nm` with routed copper counted, `gap_fixed_nm` with only fixed copper), the path that was tried, and a verdict: `contested` (fixed copper leaves room, routed copper closes the way), `rule` (a narrower legal track or the board minimum clearance passes), `placement` (nothing legal passes), `unknown` when the evidence is incomplete. The gap is a widest-path search over the router's own obstacle tests (doc 13, "The `failure` verdict"), measured at a connection's 1st, 2nd, 4th, 8th ... failure, with a deterministic budget, and it counts no work

## Alternatives

Measuring the free distance at the blocked lattice neighbour of the closest approach (it measures the approach to a wall, not the gap in it: a 0.3 mm gap with 0.2 mm clearance read 0.14 mm free); re-running the router's own search at other widths (it counts expansions against the work budget and would make a viewer change the run)

## Reason

The label has to be a number the lattice would agree with, and a viewer must not change what is routed. The unknown verdict keeps a failed search alone from sending someone to a footprint move or a clearance change (the comment on the issue)
