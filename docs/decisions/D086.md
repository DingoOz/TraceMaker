# D86 — Reported route time covers the whole job

## Decision

A route job's reported `seconds` covers the whole job, every portfolio variant included (`variant_seconds` and `variants` keep the per-variant times); `bench/run.py` with `--work` gives each board the cores per job (at most 8) as router threads

## Alternatives

Keep reporting the winner's time; fewer variants or successive halving to cut the time

## Reason

With `--work` all eight variants run whatever the thread count, and one thread ran them in series: the reported time was 1/5 to 1/8 of the process (doc 10 §6). More threads leave the output byte-identical (D47) and cut a board's wall time by 66–74 % at four threads; fewer variants change results
