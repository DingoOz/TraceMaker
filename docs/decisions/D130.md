# D130 — `--supply-first` routes dense-package supply nets first

## Decision

`--supply-first`: nets with 2+ perimeter pins on one dense package and no pour join the `--first-nets` (doc 05 §51)

## Context

The last open connections of the EtherCAT shield were all the core and 3V3 nets: routed late as pin-to-pin edges round the package, they lose to the comb; routed first they cost the others little

## Consequences and results

Off by default; the bundles stay first (after the supply nets they were worse); a supply net that needs the lane's ground stays unrouted and the closure relaxes it
