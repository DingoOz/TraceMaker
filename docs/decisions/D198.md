# D198 — Copper that joins no pad is taken off, and generated ids never repeat

## Context

Two outputs of the M27 runs were complete by the router's count and not by KiCad's: MicroMaple with four layers added (1 unconnected item) and
LFK78 at the end of the stronger ladder (2). KiCad's report named items of different nets as one missing connection.

Two faults were behind it (`bench/records/finish-2026-11`, "Complete for the router, not for KiCad"):

- **Repeated ids.** `BoardEditor` makes the ids of new tracks and vias from the seed and a counter that starts at 0. A board TraceMaker wrote
  and then routes again with the same seed (a warm start, a repair on top, every rung of `route --finish`) got the same ids a second time:
  157 repeated ids of 702 on that MicroMaple board, 899 of 5,629 on LFK78, 2,512 of 12,111 on Aleste. KiCad looks items up by id, and its
  report then described the wrong items. With unique ids the count of unconnected items was the same, so this was not the cause of the count.
- **Copper with no pad on it.** The count came from pieces of a net's copper that join no pad: on MicroMaple a via with two 0.1 mm tracks, on
  LFK78 a 40 mm run on an inner layer and a short track. They are what is left of a route when part of it is lifted or replaced. The router
  counts pads, so the board is complete for it; KiCad counts every piece of copper of a net and reports the piece as unconnected.

## Decision

- `BoardEditor::next_uuid` reads the ids already in the document (`(uuid ...)` and KiCad 6's `(tstamp ...)`) the first time it is asked, and
  skips any id it would repeat. The set is only looked up, so the output does not depend on its order. A board with no earlier TraceMaker ids
  gets the ids it got before.
- `passes::padless_islands` (called by the route job after the repair stage, before the board is written): a group of touching tracks and
  vias with no pad, zone fill, graphic, arc, locked item or free via in it is taken off when it holds copper of this run, or when one of its
  items was joined to a pad in the input as read. A group that joined no pad in the input either is the user's and stays. Links are KiCad's
  (overlapping copper of one net is joined), so nothing KiCad sees as connected is cut. The log says how many pieces went, and the summary
  has `islands_cut`.
- `--keep-islands` turns the second off.

## Alternatives

- *Find and fix every place that can leave a piece.* The repair's `prune` already follows the ends of what it lifts; the pieces found were
  copper of the run itself. A check on the finished board covers every stage, including ones added later.
- *Count the pieces as open connections in the router.* It would make the router's count agree with KiCad's, but the pieces serve nothing and
  the board is better without them.
- *Random ids.* Rule 2: the output must be the same for the same input and seed.

## Consequences

- MicroMaple's +2 layer step run again: 1 piece (a via and two tracks, all of that run) taken off; the router's 2 open and KiCad's 2
  unconnected agree (3 before).
- Quick tier: 30 of 30 outputs byte-identical (`islq` against `finq`); no board of the tier has such a piece.
- Tests: `[islands]` (new copper cut, a lift's remains cut, the input's stray copper and locked copper kept), `[editor]` (a second write with
  the same seed repeats no id).
- Open: outputs written before this change still carry repeated ids; routing them again does not repair the old ones.
