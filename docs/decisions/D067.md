# D67 — Vias kept off SMD pads narrower than 2 mm

## Decision

Vias stay off SMD pads narrower than 2 mm (`--keep-vias-off-pads`, any net, net-class clearance), exposed pads excepted

## Alternatives

Allow same-net vias in pads (the default); a separate via-in-pad cost

## Reason

Plane targets made in-pad vias the cheapest plane connection (about 100 on one board, 0.5 mm vias in 0.25 mm pins), which needs filled and capped vias and spoils fine-pitch lands. 2 mm keeps 0402/0603, QFN pins, crystals and test pads clear and lets exposed pads take thermal vias
