# D186 — One time limit for the whole run (--time-total)

## Context

M23 E6 (doc 18 §20, D183). `--time T` limits every stage on its own: the plain run, each relax rescue pass (D160, D163), each advisor round
and each repair. So a board left open took about three times T, and the KiCad plugin at `--time 60` took 325 s (`bench/records/m23-e2`).

## Decision

- `route --time-total T`: one deadline counted from the job's start. The plain run gets `--rescue-share` of T (**default 0.75**) when the
  relax rescue may follow, otherwise all of it. Each rescue pass gets what is left divided by the passes still to run. The advisor round, the
  repair windows, the refill repair and the thermal repair get at most what is left. A stage with under 1 s left is skipped and logged.
- `--time` keeps its per-stage meaning when `--time-total` is not given, so benchmarks and scripts are unchanged.
- The KiCad plugin's default becomes `--time-total 120`.
- Doc 05 §82.

## Alternatives

- **Share 0.5**: measured, 73 fewer connections than 0.75 at the same wall time (`bench/records/m23-e6`).
- **`--time T/3` in the plugin**: the same wall time as a total of T, but it routed the fewest (`--time 40` against a total of 120: −111).
- **Changing what `--time` means**: it would change every benchmark record and script that uses it; a new option is explicit.

## Consequences

At equal wall time, 15,982 of 16,935 connections are routed against 15,871 with the per-stage limit. The run now ends near its stated time
(median 124 s for a total of 120 s) instead of near three times it. It is not a hard stop: the relax pass's legalising stage does not check the
time, and 14 of the 33 boards took over 125 s (at most 148 s). The doc 18 gate ("ends within its stated time") is met only in part; bounding the
legaliser is the open part.
