# D16 — Double-pitch portfolio variants on large boards

## Decision

Large boards (≥ 3M lattice points per layer) get two portfolio variants at double pitch

## Alternatives

One pitch for all variants

## Reason

Fine-pitch lattices are time-limited on large boards (P8000: 325 → 334 of 361 in 120 s)
