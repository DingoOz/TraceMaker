# D21 — RUDY overflow and pin density as routability term

## Decision

Routability term is RUDY overflow + pin density on a ≤ 32×32 grid, integer nm, power nets excluded; the router loop shrinks bin capacity around failed connections

## Alternatives

Electrostatic `R(x)` in global placement; airwire-crossing density only

## Reason

Cheap to keep incremental and exact in the annealer (reference = from-scratch recompute); capacity shrinking is the PCB analogue of RePlAce cell inflation
