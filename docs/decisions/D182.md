# D182 — Benchmark harness stops a board at 4 x --time + 120 s

## Context

The M17 tier run of 2026-11-13 (doc 10 §11.4, D181) lost 37 of 264 board results to `bench/run.py`'s own stop, 3 × `--time` + 60 s.
`fifogfx_c64cart` alone on an idle machine with `--time 120` took 400 s (`bench/records/m17/overrun-c64cart.log`): the plain portfolio
120 s, then the relax rescue's warm and fresh passes 120 s each (D160 gives the rescue `--time` again; D163 made it two passes), then
the post-route stages. That is the engine's designed budget, not an overrun. Doc 05 §68 still said "up to twice the time".

## Decision

`bench/run.py` stops a route at 4 × `--time` + 120 s, which covers the plain run, two rescue passes and the post-route stages with
room for a loaded machine. The engine is unchanged. Doc 05 §68 now says a board left open can take three times `--time`.

## Alternatives

- **Share one `--time` between the plain run and the rescue** (an engine change): it changes every result on boards left open and
  undoes D160's reason for a budget of its own (the plain portfolio uses all its time on hard boards). Not without a tier run of its own.
- **Leave the stop as is**: boards left open get no routed count and drop out of completion, and which ones drop depends on the
  machine's load.

## Consequences

Tier runs from now on have results for boards that the old stop killed, so their board counts and completion can differ from earlier
records on the same tiers with no engine change. A run that leaves many boards open takes longer. Clean-pass numbers are unaffected,
since only boards left open reach the rescue.
