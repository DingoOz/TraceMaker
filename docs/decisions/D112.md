# D112 — Stuck report as part of the route

## Decision

The **stuck report** is part of the route: structured `RouteResult::Unrouted` (reason code, the router's words, rips, fails, pad positions, blocker nets) and a JSON report with the open connections, the crowding of their regions, the parts at their ends, the portfolio's variants, `cuts` and `escape_rings`. It carries a `disclosure` block, is size-limited (40 open connections), and `--advisor-redact` replaces net and part names by aliases (`N1`, `P3`) that are mapped back before the advice is checked. The `failure` event gets `region` and `blocker_nets`

## Alternatives

Sending the board file; sending names unconditionally; waiting for doc 06's closed-set boundary analysis before reporting blockers

## Reason

Names from a board are a prompt-injection surface and, sent to a cloud model, a disclosure; the report must say what it holds. The victims of the rejected route are the cheap and true part of "blockers" (doc 17 §3)
