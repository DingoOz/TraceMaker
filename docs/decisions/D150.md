# D150 — Vias and pads that remove unconnected copper, and tuning patterns left out of pair coupling

## Context

D149 left vme-wren's pair verdicts different from KiCad's and blamed via rings that KiCad removes on unconnected layers
(`remove_unused_layers`), which the engine did not model. The same gap gave 22,362 clearance and 21 shorting reports on vme-wren
where KiCad gives none: the engine judged rings, and filled zones around rings, that are not on the board. Looking further, two more
KiCad 10.0.6 rules in the diff-pair coupling test (`drc_test_provider_diff_pair_coupling.cpp`) were missing: tracks of a tuning
pattern are left out, and nearly parallel tracks are coupled because `SEG::Intersect` in line mode gives up when the crossing point
does not fit in 32 bits. Doc 05 §60, records in `bench/records/d150-padstacks`.

## Decision

- **Read the padstack mode** of vias and through-hole pads token by token in file order, as KiCad's parser sets it, with
  `zone_layer_connections`.
- **Flash as KiCad does** (`drc::flash_layers`): a conditional layer keeps copper where a zone joins it there or where a same-net
  track, arc, via or pad reaches its hole on that layer (`CN_VISITOR`), with KiCad's exclusions.
- **Split the copper item**: copper on the flashed layers, plus a hole-shaped bare part on the others, checked at copper clearance and
  left out of connectivity and of the copper-only checks.
- **Refill** recomputes the zone connections first, as KiCad's filler does, and knocks a same-net via that no zone joins out of the
  fill on that layer.
- **Router**: stricter than KiCad. A new track keeps clear of a same-net bare part, and a new via keeps clear of a same-net item that
  may lose copper, instead of relying on KiCad to flash the layer.
- **Tuning patterns**: members of a `(generated (type tuning_pattern))` group are marked when read and skipped by the DRC's coupling
  test and length sums and by the router's coupling test.
- **Nearly parallel tracks**: `drc::couple` computes the crossing point as `SEG::intersects` does (128-bit rescale, wrapping 64-bit
  addition) and couples the tracks when it falls outside 32 bits.

## Alternatives

- **Treat such vias as copper on every layer (as before).** Safe for the router, but the DRC reports thousands of errors KiCad does
  not, and the refill leaves voids KiCad fills.
- **Let the router flash layers it reaches, as KiCad would.** Correct, but the commit check would need to rebuild a via's shape as
  tracks arrive; four fixture boards use the option and none is in a bench tier.
- **Leave the tuning-pattern and parallel quirks out.** Each changes vme-wren's verdicts, and both are small.

## Consequences

- vme-wren: 425 clearance and 20 shorting reports left (other causes, listed in the records), and all 28 gap and 6 uncoupled-length
  errors are KiCad's. jetson still matches. The eight B1/B2 pair-rule boards route byte for byte as before.
- Rule parity: 234 cases, three new (`via_unused_layers`, `diff_pair_tuning_pattern`, `diff_pair_near_parallel`), all matching
  KiCad. The D149 build fails the three.
- Assumption A92 records the router's stricter rule.
- Still open on vme-wren: `fromTo()` (34 length errors), the pad-to-track rule resolution, copper-graphic shorts, and a 10-minute
  DRC.
