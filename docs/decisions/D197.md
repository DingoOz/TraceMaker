# D197 — Rule fit from a reference: pad mask openings its tracks cross

## Context

memsarray_mems_modules stayed one connection short on every arm. Its microphone's supply pads sit inside a ring of ground pads; the board
opens every pad's solder mask 0.2 mm wider than the copper, the openings of the ring meet at the corners, and the router keeps other
nets' tracks out of a pad's opening (KiCad's `solder_mask_bridge`). The designer's tracks go through those corners. KiCad 10.0.6 reports 7
`solder_mask_bridge` on the designer's layout and none on the unrouted board; on nine of the 33 failing boards the designer's layout has
3 to 118 (`bench/records/finish-2026-11`).

## Decision

- `--fit-rules-from REF` counts the pad mask openings of REF that a track or via of another net lies in
  (`Obstacles::mask_openings_over_tracks`). With 3 or more (the fit's evidence threshold), no pad opening keeps tracks or vias out
  (`RouterOptions::soft_pad_masks`); the log and the summary's `fitted_rules.mask_openings_over_tracks` say so.
- `--soft-pad-masks` turns the same on by hand, and `route --finish --finish-masks` makes it a rung (D196).
- `tracemaker fit-rules` prints the three mask counts.

Off by default. KiCad reports `solder_mask_bridge` where the output uses it, as on the reference.

## Alternatives

- Evidence from the board's own pads (openings of different nets that overlap): KiCad does not report those inside one footprint by
  default, so they are not evidence that the designer accepted a track in an opening. Not built.
- Soften only the openings the reference's tracks cross: the router's tracks need not take the designer's ways. Board-wide, as the
  other fitted rules are.

## Consequences

memsarray completes (10 of 10) for the first time; KiCad: 0 unconnected, no added violations on that output. On top of the fitted
result with the designer's layout as reference: sizif 38 → 26, LFK78 31 → 27, poncho and dorkyboard unchanged. With `--soft-pad-masks` on
every board still open (22 run): DronPi 9 → 2, V2X 11 → 8, TCKB 8 → 4, EtherCAT 20 → 18. A track in a pad's opening is bare copper beside
a solder joint: a judgement for the user, which is why it is never on without the reference's evidence or the option.
