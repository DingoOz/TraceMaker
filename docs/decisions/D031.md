# D31 — `--component-rules` off by default for placement and routing

!!! warning "Superseded by [D52](D052.md)"

## Decision

`--component-rules` defaults to `off` for both `tracemaker-place` and `tracemaker route`; `soft` is opt-in

## Alternatives

Default `soft` (doc 15 §3.5 planned it once the §9 gates pass)

## Reason

Measured on the 23-board placement set, 9 ESD boards and 6 boards × 3 seeds (doc 15 §14.3): `soft` brings crystals 6.4 → 5.1 mm, ESD parts 7.1 → 3.2 mm and load caps closer at equal HPWL, but decoupling and regulator caps move away on some boards (decaps worse on 9 of 18 runs); doubling the decap ties fixes that for +2–3 % HPWL. Held-out detection recall for USB connectors, connectors and mounting holes is below the 0.8 gate. `off` reproduces the previous output byte for byte
