# D96 — SRJ fan-out import options for `fanout31`

## Decision

Simple Route JSON fan-out problems (tscircuit's `fanout31`) are imported with: plane terminations (`buses[].termination`) as zones, breakout points on the layer `connectionExitTargets` names, `--unpointed-keepout` (pads of a net that no connection point lies on are keep-outs), `--outline-margin-mm`, and the extension field `tracemakerTrackLayers` (layers outside it get a rule area that forbids tracks and lets vias through). Clearance is the stated track width, 0.08128 mm

## Alternatives

Model the far chip's pads as part of the problem (rejected: it asks for 149 connections the benchmark does not score)

## Reason

The problem asks for the fan-out at the SoC only and its nets name the far chip's pads; without these the first case routed 51 of 149 connections, with them 100 of 100. The clearance sits between the stated trace-to-pad 0.05 mm and pad-to-pad 0.1 mm (doc 08 §9; doc 16 §10)
