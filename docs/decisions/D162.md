# D162 — The realizer on by default: the legaliser's last hand-over solved as a QP

## Context

Doc 20 §2.3, step 3 of the plan for 100 % routed boards. Relax's routes reach the board's rules through the projection legaliser, pair
by pair. Where a bundle of tracks has to move together, the projection stalls: poncho's last hand-over leaves 71 pairs, the same after
3000 sweeps, although the cut test (D159) finds room. Those routes are dropped, and relax loses connections the plain run keeps.

## Decision

- **The realizer** (`route/legalize.cpp`, doc 05 §70). After the sweeps, when pairs are left: sequential quadratic programming. It
  linearises the constraints near their target and solves the elastic QP for the smallest move that meets them all, within each
  node's box and a trust region. The QP is solved by dual coordinate ascent (Hildreth 1957) in a fixed row order. A round is kept only
  if the exact recheck finds fewer pairs, or as many less deep.
- **Judged by the routes kept** (`route/router.cpp`). The router also legalises by projection alone and keeps the realizer's result
  only if the exact check's conflict-free subset keeps more of its routes; a tie keeps the projection's. Judging by the pairs alone
  cost uC3Moy one route (9 → 5 pairs, 40 → 39 kept) and failed the quick tier's gate.
- **On by default:** `LegalizeOptions::realize_iters = 20`. `TM_LEGALIZE_REALIZE=N` overrides it for experiments (0 turns it off).
- `TM_LEGALIZE_DUMP=DIR` writes the legaliser's problem as JSON when pairs are left, for the prototypes.

## Alternatives

- **Linear programs with a trust region** (scipy HiGHS prototype): as good as the QP on poncho (7 pairs left), but it needs an LP
  solver in the engine.
- **A penalty method (L-BFGS-B) and an augmented Lagrangian:** they settle into a compromise in the squeezed ladder (92 pairs left).
- **More sweeps or a larger move box for the projection:** no change at 3000 sweeps.
- **Shortest homotopic paths with spacing** (doc 20 §2.3): more general, days of work. Not needed for the clusters seen.

## Consequences

- Poncho's last hand-over: 71 → 7 pairs.
- The 33 failing PCBench tier B-D boards under `--relax`: 15,940 against 15,925 routed (+15, 5 better, 0 worse), 5 clean boards either
  way, no DRC error added. srj20's six stuck boards under `--relax`: 411 against 404 (+7, 4 better, 0 worse).
- The two `--relax` measurements were made while the realizer was judged by its pairs. srj20's six, run again with the routes-kept rule:
  411, board for board the same. The quick tier (with D163's two rescue passes): +89 connections against D160, 17 boards better, 0
  worse, 16 clean against 12, gates pass.
- The default run gets it through the relax rescue (D160). Plain routing has no legaliser and is unchanged.
- CPU: within the machine's noise (+12 % on one set, −13 % on the other).
- Records: `bench/records/complete-routing/runs`, `bench/records/complete-routing/realizer`. Docs: doc 05 §70, doc 20 §2.3 and §4.
