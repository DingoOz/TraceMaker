# D194 — Rule fit: relax the rules a board's own copper already breaks

## Context

Asked why some boards never reach 100 % when rules may be relaxed, the designer-oracle test (doc 20 §1.1,
`bench/records/oracle-2026-11`) found boards on which the router cannot put back the designer's own nets inside the designer's
own layout. Those boards break, as designed, rules the router is held to: solder-mask openings that already expose another
net's pad (card-edge fingers on both sides, solder jumpers), copper-to-edge and hole clearances that the KiCad 5 file format never
stored (KiCad 10's defaults hold), minimum widths, and class values wider than the designer drew. 141 of 261 open connections
on 21 boards do not route with every other route lifted.

The earlier rule relaxation (the closure loop of the real-world study, doc 10) lowered clearances and widths tier by tier for the
nets in the way. It closed about 7 of 47 boards: it relaxed rules that were not what blocked the board.

## Decision

`route --fit-rules` relaxes the rules that the board's own copper already breaks, to what that copper has, and reports every
change (`src/drc/fit_rules.{hpp,cpp}`, doc 20 §2.8). Off by default.

- The reference is the input board, or `--fit-rules-from BOARD` (an earlier layout of the same board).
- Minimums (copper-to-edge, hole clearance, track width, annular ring, via diameter, drill) come down to the smallest value the
  reference's DRC violations measure. The class clearance comes down to the low decile of the measured gaps and not under
  `--fit-clearance-floor` (0.09 mm). Fewer than three violations of a kind fit nothing.
- With tracks on the reference, a class's track width comes down to the width carrying half its track length there, and its via to
  the most used via of the class there.
- A pad's mask opening that already exposes another net's pad no longer keeps tracks out
  (`RouterOptions::soft_bridged_masks`); mask openings drawn over pads turn on `--soft-mask-graphics` (D127).
- The DRC records the measured gap of copper-to-edge and hole-clearance violations (`Violation::actual`), which the fit reads.
  KiCad's report layout is unchanged.
- `tracemaker fit-rules BOARD [--from REF]` prints the fit without routing. The summary carries `fitted_rules`.

## Alternatives

- **Let a project file override the defaults on a KiCad 5 board:** KiCad itself ignores it there, and rule 8 and the judge follow
  KiCad. A user who wants other minimums can save the board in the current format. The fit needs no file.
- **Relax by tier until the board routes** (the closure loop): measured before; it relaxes where the congestion is, not where the
  board is inconsistent, and it does not touch the mask openings.
- **Fit the clearance to the smallest gap:** one slip (a pair 0.01 mm apart on sizif512ext) took every class to the floor. The low
  decile with a minimum of three violations is what is built.
- **Make the mask openings soft always:** it changes what KiCad reports on boards that are clean today. The evidence rule (the
  opening already exposes another net's pad on the input) limits it to boards that already have the error.
- **On by default:** three to five of 33 boards get worse (LeeChee 5 → 22 open, 58r-mxalps 0 → 5, poncho 33 → 45), and the output
  breaks the stated rules. It is a rung of the ladder that keeps the better result, for a user who accepts the relaxation.

## Consequences

- 33 failing PCBench boards, from nothing, 20 M work: 890 open and 3 complete without; 771 and 6 fitted to the board's own pads;
  734 and 8 fitted to the designer's layout; 700 and 9 taking the better of plain and fitted per board. fifogfx, test-jig, PCIE,
  ErgoDone, sd_wav and OtterPill complete for the first time, with 0 unconnected and no short in KiCad's judgement.
- The fitted output carries KiCad errors of the fitted kinds (fifogfx 41 solder_mask_bridge, test-jig 167 clearance and 38
  solder_mask_bridge): the designer's layouts carry the same kinds.
- The copper-to-edge value is one number for the board: fingers on the edge let tracks reach the edge anywhere
  (`--fit-edge-floor`).
- Not addressed: pin escapes with no slack (sizif's QFN), which are a lattice loss and not a rule; the search boards (doc 20 §2.9).
- With the options off the route is unchanged (quick tier, `bench/records/oracle-2026-11`). Tests: `tests/test_fit_rules.cpp`.
- Docs: doc 20 §1.1, §2.8-§2.10; `docs/reference/cli.md` regenerated.
