# D199 — One search's memory is limited

## Context

PCBench mini_mass_prog_bench_prog_rig (646 x 155 mm, pitch 0.065 mm) exhausted a 184 GB machine during the M27 runs. A search keeps one
16-byte record per state of its window: cells x layers x 9 direction states. A connection across that board on eight layers asks for 27 GB
in one allocation, in every variant of the portfolio at once; with a 20 GB limit on the process it ends with `std::bad_alloc` three seconds
after it starts (traced with `strace -k`: `Router::Impl::search`, `sn.assign`). From nothing on six layers the same board peaks at 0.7 GB,
because its first connections are short.

## Decision

`RouterOptions::search_mem_mb` (`route --search-mem MB`, default 4096, 0 = no limit) bounds the state records of one search:

1. a window over the limit is searched with one direction state per point (what the fast-bends variants do everywhere): nine times less,
   and nothing of the board is left out;
2. if that is still over, the window is drawn in towards the box of the connection's two ends until it fits;
3. if that box alone is over, the search does not start and the connection stays open.

The run prints how many searches each of the three touched; `RouteResult::search_mem_limited` has the sum. The limit is a fixed number, not
the machine's free memory, so the same job gives the same board everywhere (rule 2).

## Alternatives

- *Read the free memory and adapt.* The result would depend on the machine and on what else runs.
- *Sparse state (a hash map of visited states).* The right structure for a window this large, and a larger change to the search's inner
  loop; left for later.
- *Fewer variants at once on large boards.* It lowers the total and leaves the single 27 GB allocation.

## Consequences

- The mini_mass case (eight layers, on top of its six-layer result, one variant) runs to the end under a 20 GB limit with a peak of 10.3 GB;
  16 searches ran with one direction state. Its 7 open connections stay open.
- 4096 MB allows 3.7 M cells of window on eight layers with bend states (about 125 x 125 mm at 0.065 mm) and 33 M without.
- Quick tier: 30 of 30 outputs byte-identical; no search of the tier reaches the limit.
- Test: `[search-mem]` (a 1 MB limit: both connections routed, the counter set, the result the same twice).
- Open: the other per-window arrays (about 6 GB in that case) are not limited, and eight variants still multiply the total.
