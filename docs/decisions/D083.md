# D83 — `tracemaker escape` analyses outstanding pins under router rules

## Decision

`tracemaker escape` analyses outstanding pins only (nets the existing copper does not complete; complete ones are `satisfied`), under the router's custom-rule limits (per-net layers and vias, positional disallow rules) and its zone and via options (`--soft-zones`, `--keep-vias-off-pads`). Dead pins that escape without the rules are named as walled in by them

## Alternatives

Port the fork's shared off-lattice access graph with witness/exhausted/unknown verdicts (D66 there); keep analysing every pin against hard fills

## Reason

The analysis feeds preflight and `dead_pins`: it must ask the router's question. Retained fills made 18 CM5 pins look dead that soft-zone routing connects. The access graph was dropped with its router half; the lattice search is complete over its domain, so `dead` keeps its meaning, qualified by the reported lattice (doc 05 §35)
