# D184 — Power nets routed wide from the start (--power-width)

## Context

M23 E1 (doc 18 §20, D183). Routed boards had power at signal width: robomezzi at 0.25 mm where the designer drew 0.6 to 2.5 mm. 314 of
the PCBench boards under 800 kB have designers who drew at least half of their supply copper at 0.5 mm or wider. `--widen-power` (D092)
widens only after routing, where signals have left room.

## Decision

`tracemaker route --power-width MM` (off by default):
- supply nets by name (`supply_net_name`, doc 05 §79) get MM as their target unless `--widen-net` or a component rule states one;
- every net with a target wider than its class width is first searched strictly at the target (`RouterOptions::preferred_widths`), then
  routed as usual at its class width if that fails;
- wide tracks are necked to the narrower pads they end in (`passes::neck_at_pads`, only 0/45/90° splits, so copper only shrinks);
- `--widen-power` runs after.

## Alternatives

- **Widen after routing only** (D092 as it was): leaves power narrow where signals took the room first (base arm: 20 % of supply copper at
  0.5 mm, against 94 % with this).
- **Wide attempts in the first pass only**: the same routed counts on the 9 open boards, less width (91 %). Dropped.
- **`crules::power_like_name` as the supply test**: it also takes sense lines and KiCad's pin-derived names. A stricter test is used.
- **On by default**: −2 of 4,038 connections (two boards +3, two −5) is close to the doc 18 §20.2 gate but not equal. It stays an option,
  and E2 offers it as a plugin setting.

## Consequences

On the power-wide set, supply copper at ≥ 0.5 mm goes from 20 % to 94 % (the designers: 84 %), clean boards stay 21, no error is added,
and CPU drops 2.9 % at the same work. Routed: −2 of 4,038. Records: `bench/records/m23-e1`. Docs: doc 05 §79, doc 18 §20.
