3// Fan-out heads (M22 item 8, D157; design doc 05 §65): the first millimetres of each perimeter pin's route out of a dense
4// four-sided package, laid as the designer lays them before anything else is routed.
5//
6// On mini_mass_prog_bench_prog_rig the designer's own copper within 5 mm of the QFN-44 (only the parts that touch one pad),
7// put into the unrouted board, let the router reach 990 of 995 connections where it otherwise stops at 986: the other nets
8// route round the escapes instead of putting a ring of vias 1-2 mm out that boxes the pins in. Straight stubs from every pin
9// did worse (983); what the designer does is to leave each side either straight or in staggered 45-degree bundles toward the
10// pins' destinations, and to keep a side straight where the bundles would run into the parts beside the package.
11//
12// The heads are planar by construction (the ordered escape of Kong, Yan and Wong, "Optimal simultaneous pin assignment and
13// escape routing for dense PCBs", ASP-DAC 2010, restricted to one bend per pin): along a side the pins split into a block that
14// turns toward the low end, a straight block and a block that turns toward the high end, and each turning pin bends one step
15// further out than its neighbour toward the corner, so parallel diagonals keep track width plus clearance apart.
16// Every leg is cut at the first point the exact check refuses; a head that ran into something stops short of it, so its own
17// route keeps room to leave.

25struct FanoutHeadOptions {

50// Plans and adds (through hooks.add) the heads of every qualifying package. Pins that get no head: nets with a zone, pins whose
51// net has no pad off the package, and pins whose nearest such pad lies within `reach` of the package (local connections are the
52// router's). A head never comes within clearance of another pad or other copper of its own net, so it joins nothing: the
53// connection plan is unchanged, and removing the heads after routing cannot disconnect a route (each runs pad to pad).
54// Deterministic: footprints in board order, sides in a fixed order, pins by position.
