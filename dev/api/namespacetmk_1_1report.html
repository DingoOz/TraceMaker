◆ drc_json()

nlohmann::json tmk::report::drc_json ( const model::Board & before,
const model::Board & after,
const model::DesignRules & rules,
bool refill )

TraceMaker's DRC (KiCad's violation names, doc 03 §6) of the board the router started from and of the board it wrote, by violation type, with what the route added.

With refill the copper zones of both are refilled first, as kicad-cli pcb drc --refill-zones does (the plane tier is judged that way, doc 10 §4). Counts per type, not the instance matching of bench/drc_judge.py: a type whose count did not grow may still hold a new violation that replaced an old one.

What the router took from the board's project (board_path names the .kicad_pcb; the .kicad_pro and .kicad_dru are looked for beside it as io::read_design_rules does): board minimums, net classes with their net counts, custom rules, and everything that could not be read (rules.warnings, the rule engine's engine_warnings and the board reader's board.warnings).

Rule 6: what is in not_read the route may break.
