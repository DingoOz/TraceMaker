◆ keep_overlaps

bool tmk::place::ClosureOptions::keep_overlaps = false

M24 (D192): a part that overlaps others in the input may move if, where it goes, it overlaps only parts whose courtyards it overlapped there (input_pairs = keepable_pairs, each (a, b) with a < b) and its holes and copper stay clear of them, so no new pair of overlapping parts appears.

Such moves rank after the strictly legal ones. Off: a moved part must be free of every conflict (D190), which leaves no move for parts a hand placement has pinned in an overlap.
