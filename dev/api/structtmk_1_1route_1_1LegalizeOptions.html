◆ subdivide_mm

double tmk::route::LegalizeOptions::subdivide_mm = 0

> 0: every unlocked route track is cut into pieces of at most this length (at the vertices of same-net copper that lie on it, and evenly in between), so that a long straight track can bend where its neighbours are too close, instead of only moving as a whole; after the sweeps the vertices that stayed on a straight line are merged again.

0 = the vertices the routes have (unchanged behaviour).
