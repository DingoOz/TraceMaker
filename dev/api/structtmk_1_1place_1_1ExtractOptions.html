◆ copper_only

std::vector<std::string> tmk::place::ExtractOptions::copper_only

References tested by copper and holes only: neither their outline nor the other parts' courtyards count against them (copper clearance, hole spacing and the board edge still do).

The last resort before a part is left off the board: battery holders, modules and headers that the designer placed across other courtyards.

std::vector<std::string> tmk::place::ExtractOptions::pads_only
