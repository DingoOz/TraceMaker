◆ apply_out

std::string tmk::app::ChainsOptions::apply_out

Not only measured: the chains that close are put on the board, one open connection after the other (each on the board the ones before left), and the board is written here (doc 20 §2.16, D200).

It is written only when fewer connections are open than on the input and every net that was lifted is joined as it was; copper left with no pad on it is taken off (D198).
