◆ infer_graphic_nets

bool tmk::model::Board::infer_graphic_nets = false

Board-level copper graphics (polygons on a copper layer) take the net of the one net whose pads, tracks or vias they touch (a designer's manual pour); ones touching several nets stay net-less.

Off: every board copper graphic is net-less copper that no track may touch (route –soft-copper-graphics turns it on).
