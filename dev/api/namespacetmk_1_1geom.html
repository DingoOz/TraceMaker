◆ fracture()

Ring tmk::geom::fracture ( const Polygon & p)

One ring with zero-width bridges from every hole to the outline, as KiCad writes a zone's filled_polygon (SHAPE_POLY_SET::Fracture).

Holes are joined in order of their leftmost point, each by a horizontal bridge to the nearest edge on its left (the hole-elimination step of ear clipping; Eberly, 2002).
