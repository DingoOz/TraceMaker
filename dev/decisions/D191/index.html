    D191 — M26: finish srj20's last BGA boards with a reserved fanout¶

    Context¶

    After the srj20 ramp campaigns of 2026-11-15 to 11-17 (bench/records/srj20-ramp-2026-11), 189 of 200 srj20 boards had been routed clean at least once, and the other eleven were fixed points: warm restarts, looser starts, seeds, --lns-open, --island-slack, --ramp-fresh, --first-nets and --layer-dir-pen did not move them. The user asked how a person would finish them, then for each idea to become a feature in a new milestone. Pictures of every open connection showed the same blockage: 95 of 105 open balls have no free dog-bone via site, the boards have 3-4 vias per connection with few on the sites, 38 open balls are outer-ring balls boxed in from outside, and the open boards have the most crossing connections of the 200 (doc 18 §21.1).

    Decision¶

    Plan milestone M26 (doc 11, doc 18 §21.2) with nine items, F1-F9. F8 (the best board includes a legal warm start) is built first, as a rule-4 fix. F1 then measures where the sites are lost. The core is F2, a dog-bone site per ball from the escape plan's site flow, closed to other nets' vias in every search until its ball connects, together with F3, a via-free band round the package. F4-F7 (move a blocking via, fewer vias per connection, lanes under the package, long crossing connections first) are measured on top, and F9 reports boards that need more layers. Every option starts off. The gate is in doc 11.

    Alternatives¶

    • More runs of the existing recipe. Rejected: the open counts did not move across the campaigns.
    • A stronger layer-direction penalty. Measured and rejected: worse on 040, 152 and 070 (doc 18 §21.1).
    • Version 2's full channel corridors (doc 05 §12). Kept opt-in. Reserving long corridors lost on logicbone. F2 keeps only its site flow, and F6 turns the lanes into a cost bonus.
    • Routing the always-open nets first alone. Measured: those nets get routed and others fail. Kept only as F7, after the sites are reserved.

    Consequences¶

    F8 was built first (doc 05 §84): the warm start is the ramp's floor, turned off in the relax rescue, which already keeps the better result. The tune/report split follows doc 16 §1 (tune on boards 1-100). F2 changes the escape plan's corridor semantics under negotiated searches, so it must stay off by default until the gate is met. Docs changed: 11 (M26 row) and 18 (§21).

    F1, F2, F3 and F5 were then measured on 152, 070 and 040 (doc 18 §21). Sites are lost mostly to tracks, not vias; hard reservations and via-cost guidance both left more open than the control from nothing, and no option moved the 2-open warm boards. F2 and F3 (hard) and F5 (dearer via) are not adopted; the options already in the router were enough to test them, so no new code was added for them.

    F4 (--via-shove, already built) kept none of 427 moves. F6 (--package-lanes) and F7 (--order 4, --order-all) were built as options and lost to the control, so they stay off (doc 05 §85-§86). F9 (doc 05 §87) is adopted: line_crossings in every summary and a two-layer hint at 11.5 crossings per connection. The gate (≥ 3 of the eleven clean) is not met. With the options off, quick and mid are byte-identical, so the default route is unchanged except the summary's new field. Docs changed: 05 (§84-§87).
