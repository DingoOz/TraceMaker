3// Critical nets, from what the board's parts are (issue #24, docs/05-routing.md §44, D117).
4//
5// JLCPCB's first rule of routing is to route the critical signals first. Which signals are critical is not in the netlist, but the component rules
6// (doc 15) know the roles the nets play: the data lines of a USB port, the pairs of an Ethernet PHY, a crystal's two pins, the clock of a memory
7// interface. This turns the roles into tiers: 1 clocks, crystals and RF lines (the signals that radiate and that nothing may disturb), 2 high-speed
8// differential links, 3 memory and other fast parallel buses. A tier is the order of routing; the user's own list (`--critical-nets`) is tier 1.
