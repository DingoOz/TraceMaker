Moves footprint index to the other side of the board the way KiCad does (FOOTPRINT::Flip, top/bottom, about its own origin: local y and pad orientations negated, text angles 180° − a, F.

  • <-> B.* layers, texts mirrored), then puts it at pos with the final orientation angle in degrees (flipped orientation −angle0 plus any rotation). Only for footprints where flip_supported() holds.

Moves footprint index to pos with orientation angle (degrees).
